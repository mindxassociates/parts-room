Connect directly in three steps

One person creates a connection offer. The other returns an answer. The Parts Room then synchronizes through an encrypted browser-to-browser data channel.

1. Create

Generate an offer and send the complete code through an agreed private channel.

2. Respond

The other person pastes it, creates an answer, and sends the complete answer back.

3. Explore

Paste the answer to connect. Add parts, notice relationships, and let meaning emerge through conversation.
